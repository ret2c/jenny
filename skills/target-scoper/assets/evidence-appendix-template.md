## Verified entrypoint anchors

For every GOAL lane, record: lane, exact current tag/commit/artifact identity,
path or service/protocol route, symbol or handler, direct official source URL,

public; then provide an exact binary/service/protocol anchor instead of a
guessed filename. Historical paths are leads until reconfirmed here.

sibling hypothesis. Do not turn the list into a mandatory hunt queue.

expected sizes, license/account/hardware prerequisites, supported topology, and
the smallest current-version lab. A landing page or sales form is not artifact
access.

## Economic evidence

likely proof cost, likely payout, ceiling, chain value, and why promising roots
are distinct or likely consolidated. Do not impose a numeric A-tier promotion
budget; same-product count is collision evidence, not a cap. Keep this private.

HUNT PROFILE POLICY

The acknowledged profile narrows the active GOAL.md; it never expands target,
lab, replay, package, submission, or lifecycle authority. CVSS means the score
supported by demonstrated impact, not a theoretical ceiling. Economic value is
a separate conservative judgment.

A_TIER_ONLY
- Intensive work requires demonstrated CVSS 8.0 or higher and credible likely
  value of at least $10,000, or an exceptional direct enterprise boundary such
  as pre-authentication RCE, sandbox escape, or equivalent control-plane
  compromise.
- A chain component is eligible only when tied to one named outcome credibly
  worth at least $10,000.
- Record lower-value leads briefly and BANK them.

BALANCED
- Intensive work requires demonstrated CVSS 7.0 or higher and credible likely
  value of at least $5,000.
- Meaningful components of a named A-tier chain are eligible.
- Record smaller B-tier leads and BANK them. Packaging a B-tier candidate still
  requires a named, candidate-bound operator exception.

INCLUDE_B_TIER
- Intensive work requires demonstrated CVSS 6.0 or higher, meaningful
  enterprise impact, and credible paid value.
- Full proof work is allowed. Once acknowledged for the matching active target,
  INCLUDE_B_TIER is standing operator authority for TIER_B_EXCEPTION. Candidate
  Challenge still independently verifies technical proof and hard eligibility;
  the standing authority may override only a PARTIAL economic review. Do not request a second candidate-specific
  approval. A named candidate-bound exception remains available when another
  profile is active.

CHECKPOINT RULES
- A selection applies at Hunter's next semantic checkpoint after the current
  guarded replay, package or Final rework transaction, and already-submitted
  Candidate Challenge reach a safe boundary.
- A loosening change is safe once the current guarded command returns. Apply it
  before starting the next guarded command; it must not remain pending across
  two semantic check-ins.
- A profile change never rewrites GOAL.md or requires a goal reread by itself.
- Finish in-flight work under its recorded prior revision. Preserve evidence
  before selecting the next tranche.
- Record the exact acknowledged revision and preset in HUNTER_STATE.md.
- Tightening preserves lower-value leads. Loosening does not automatically
  revive, challenge, number, package, or schedule BANKED or terminal work.
- If no lane is eligible, record NO_ELIGIBLE_TRANCHE and request operator
  direction. Do not silently lower the profile or park the target.
- When CVSS is not demonstrated yet, select lanes using Expected class and the
  conservative likely-value band. Candidate Challenge applies the demonstrated
  threshold before intensive proof or packaging continues.
- If policy storage is unavailable, keep the last acknowledged profile and
  reject new policy writes. Failure never makes the policy more permissive.

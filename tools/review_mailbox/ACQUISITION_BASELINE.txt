ZDI ACQUISITION BASELINE

This is the canonical standing buyer-fit baseline for target scoping, Candidate
Challenge, portfolio admission, and Final Review. Technical validity alone is
not acquisition fit.

Required:

- The vulnerability exists in the latest available shipped stable version.
- The affected product has widespread deployment.

Preference:

- Remote code execution.
- Software affecting enterprises.
- Server-side software.
- Desktop or mobile operating systems.
- Browsers.
- SCADA or IIoT.
- Sandbox escapes.
- VM escapes.
- Security products.

Normally excluded:

- Cross-site scripting.
- DLL planting.
- Live websites.
- ActiveX.
- Most consumer-only products; a widely used security product or some IoT
  products may survive only when the dossier proves the narrow exception.
- Beta or pre-release software.
- Denial-of-service-only findings.
- Anything already publicly posted or otherwise known.

These are acquisition gates in addition to currentness, proof, negative
controls, duplicate review, remediation independence, and conservative impact.
Tier-B or operator-exception authority cannot waive latest-version,
deployment, public-status, or excluded-class failures.

JENNY PORTFOLIO ADMISSION POLICY

Purpose

Technical validity is not portal worthiness. Hunt broadly, preserve meaningful
Tier-B work, and spend package/reviewer/operator attention only on candidates
that deserve promotion now.

Private candidate dispositions

- PROMOTE: build and register an external package now.
- BANK: preserve complete private evidence, reproduction, currentness, and next
  action, but do not assign a package number or build an external package.
- CONSOLIDATE: retain the candidate as a sibling or chain component and identify
  the lead root/impact it strengthens.
- WRITE_OFF: public, duplicate, fixed, stale, unsupported, or economically
  unsellable. Preserve the decision privately.

Promotion standard

PROMOTE requires all of the following:

1. Exact-current supported product identity, credible widespread deployment,
   and a technically admissible candidate. The latest shipped stable version
   must contain the issue.
2. Conservative likely payout and separate theoretical ceiling, calibrated
   against actual accepted offers and adverse buyer evidence.
3. A current same-product inventory and an independently fixable root that is
   materially distinct from submitted, accepted, held, dead, ready, and active
   siblings.
4. One of:
   - A_TIER: credible likely value around USD 10,000 or more, or a direct
     unauthenticated/low-position crossing of a major enterprise boundary.
   - CHAIN_COMPONENT: required to demonstrate one credible combined A-tier
     impact, with the exact chain and missing proof recorded.
   - TIER_B_EXCEPTION: unusually clean, distinct work with credible likely value
     around USD 5,000 or more, promoted only by explicit operator exception.
5. A concrete explanation of why this candidate should consume attention now.
6. The candidate is the strongest presently supported same-root outcome, or
   every credible stronger same-root path has a recorded evidence-backed
   typed technical closure. Absence of proof, untested geometry, harness
   friction, elapsed time, or failure to find a stable object is not closure.
   An open upgrade path remains research work and cannot be promoted.

Standing ZDI buyer-fit baseline

The canonical full rule is ACQUISITION_BASELINE.txt in this directory.

- Prefer remote code execution, enterprise/server software, desktop or mobile
  operating systems, browsers, SCADA/IIoT, sandbox or VM escapes, and security
  products.
- Do not promote XSS, DLL planting, live-site-only issues, ActiveX, ordinary
  consumer-only products, beta or pre-release software, DoS-only findings, or
  anything already public or otherwise known.
- A widely used security product or some IoT products may survive the consumer
  filter only when the dossier proves the narrow exception and deployment.
- These are acquisition gates in addition to technical validity. Tier-B
  authority cannot waive latest-version, deployment, public-status, or
  excluded-class failures.

Tier-B work remains valid research. It defaults to BANK, not suppression.
Post-authentication disclosure, ordinary authorization issues, CVSS below 7,
limited integrity impact, and DoS-only work do not PROMOTE unless a demonstrated
stronger chain or explicit operator exception changes the economics.
An acknowledged INCLUDE_B_TIER profile is standing operator authority to
promote a matching-target Tier-B candidate without another approval request.
Under another profile, the operator may explicitly direct Hunter to package a
named Tier-B candidate through a candidate-bound exception. Record the current
authority privately as TIER_B_EXCEPTION with operator_exception true and
PROMOTE. Either path changes portfolio disposition and economic review only.
Technical proof and hard eligibility remain PASS; neither path waives
supported
reachability, currentness, public or duplicate status, evidence quality, or
package-integrity gates.

A-tier standing authority

A distinct A_TIER candidate has standing authority to enter Candidate
Challenge regardless of how many same-product candidates or packages already
exist. There is no numeric promotion budget, fourth-case approval, saturation
exception, or other count-based operator decision. When independent review
confirms technical proof, hard eligibility, and economic review all PASS, the required disposition is
ADMIT_PROOF, followed by package construction. The operator is not asked to
re-authorize that normal path.

This is not a waiver. Exact public or local duplication, common remediation,
unsupported reachability, failed proof, overstated economics, or another
admission failure still requires CONSOLIDATE, WRITE_OFF, or corrected
classification.
The no-cap rule prevents package count from substituting for those evidence
decisions.

Product pressure

- Rank every candidate against the complete same-product portfolio.
- Lead with the strongest one to three candidates.
- Same-product count and concentration must be recorded, but never require
  separate operator approval for a distinct A_TIER candidate.
- More than five related cases, or a dense common-remediation family, should be
  flagged for operator awareness and exact duplicate/remediation analysis. It
  does not by itself block A-tier Candidate Challenge or packaging.
- Independently fixable roots may remain separate, but stagger the strongest
  first. One narrow fix that closes multiple candidates is consolidation
  pressure, not a reason to multiply reports.

Stage responsibilities

- Scoper defines the target-specific A-tier objective, likely bands,
  saturation evidence, and banking/chain rules. It must not create a numeric
  A-tier promotion budget or same-product approval cap.
- Hunter creates a private hash-bound portfolio-admission record before
  begin-package-build. Only PROMOTE proceeds to external packaging.
- Midlane remains a technical and mechanical reviewer. It may identify sibling
  overlap but does not decide portfolio economics.
- Final Reviewer independently decides both technical readiness and whether the
  package should be submitted now. READY means both are satisfied.
- The operator alone submits, overrides a disposition, or authorizes a Tier-B
  exception.

The portfolio record and all economics are private. They never enter external
package bytes or ZDI/signoff.txt.
